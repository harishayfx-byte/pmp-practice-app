PMP® High-Yield Exam Formulas & Whiteboard

Earned Value Management (EVM)

CV: EV - AC (Cost Variance)

SV: EV - PV (Schedule Variance)

CPI: EV / AC (>1 Under Budget)

SPI: EV / PV (>1 Ahead of Schedule)

EAC: BAC / CPI (typical)

ETC: EAC - AC

VAC: BAC - EAC

TCPI: (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC)

Schedule & Communications

Beta (PERT): (O + 4M + P) / 6

Triangular: (O + M + P) / 3

Std Dev (σ): (P - O) / 6

Variance: ((P - O) / 6)²

Float (Slack): LS - ES or LF - EF

Comm Channels: N × (N - 1) / 2
